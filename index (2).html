A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your next revision.

Is Timasla ready to use?+

This is a landing page and interactive product preview. Accounts, project storage, AI analysis, and client approval links are not live yet.

Who is it for?+

The first version is focused on freelance video editors and small video editing studios.

Will my clients need an account?+

The planned approval flow uses a private shareable link, so clients can review a specific change request without creating an account. This feature is not connected in this preview.

Does AI decide what I charge?+

No. The planned analyzer will suggest classifications and flag possible scope changes. You will review the requests and confirm all prices, timing, and classifications.

How much will it cost?+

Pricing has not been announced. This preview does not collect payments or start subscriptions.
